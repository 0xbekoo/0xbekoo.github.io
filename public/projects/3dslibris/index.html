3dsLibris

A homebrew ebook reader for Nintendo 3DS focused on reflowable and fixed-layout formats.

3dsLibris is a homebrew ebook reader for Nintendo 3DS.

It focuses on making long-form reading usable on a dual-screen handheld device, with support for formats such as EPUB, CBZ and PDF.

The project explores constrained-device UI, text layout, caching, fixed-layout rendering, and performance-sensitive C/C++ development.
